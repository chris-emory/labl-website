# Language Biomarker Lab (LaBL)

> Official website of the **Language Biomarker Lab** at **Emory University** — showcasing our research at the intersection of language, cognition, and artificial intelligence.

---

## Overview

The **Language Biomarker Lab (LaBL)** investigates how language reflects cognitive and mental health states through the power of **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** and **AI**.  
This site introduces our research initiatives, people, and projects, while visually expressing the Lab’s mission and scientific identity.

Built with:
- **Next.js 14** — App Router architecture
- **Tailwind CSS** — modern, responsive styling
- **Framer Motion** — smooth animations & micro-interactions
- **Vercel / Static Export** — fast, secure deployment
